================================
Most of the following features are stored in "assets\minecraft\optifine\random\entity" folder in this pack, can try removing the ones you don't want. 
================================

- brown bear
in any non-natural spawning biomes for polar bears

- black & white, brown cow
random variants, warm biomes tend to have more brown variants

- slightly darker green creeper
random variants

- brown frog
random variants for temperate biome frogs

- swapped warm and cold biome frog variants
rename the frog textures in the "assets\minecraft\textures\entity\frog" folder if you want it reverted back to vanilla

- dirty hoglin
random variants

- muddy pig or hairy boar
random variants, colder biomes tend to have more hairy boars

random variants

random variants
