================================
To remove the following features, delete the quoted .json files located in "assets\minecraft\blockstates" folder in this pack.
================================

- stalactite models 
random stalactite models for stone, andersite, diorite, granite, and netherrack blocks
"stone.json andesite.json diorite.json granite.json netherrack.json"

- corpse piles
random piles ontop of nether quartz ore blocks
"nether_quartz_ore.json"

- 3d crystals
random 3d crystals for redstone, emerald, and diamond ore/deepslate ore blocks
"redstone_ore.json emerald_ore.json diamond_ore.json deepslate_redstone_ore.json deepslate_emerald_ore.json deepslate_diamond_ore.json"

- small basalt collumns
random quarter collumns for basalt blocks
"basalt.json"

- extra grass
random extra grass ontop of grass blocks, and also hanging from the sides
"grass_block.json"

- small bushes
grass will randomly use birch/jungle leaves block as small bushes
"short_grass.json"

- crop height variation
various farm crops will have some height variation
"beetroots.json carrots.json potatoes.json wheat.json"

- hanging roots
random short hanging roots below grass, podzol, and rooted dirt block
"grass_block.json podzol.json rooted_dirt.json"

- lilypad roots
random roots below lilypads
"lily_pad.json"
