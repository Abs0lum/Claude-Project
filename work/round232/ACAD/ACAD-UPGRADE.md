# ACADEMY I → II → III: the upgrade path (round 232, item #24, his 11:27 priority)

**Status:** this is a design proposal. Nothing is built. `academygen` has not been started (castle doc §12, row 24). Every
number marked **PROPOSAL** needs his ruling. The note is measured on the Academy II v2 layout *after* item #23 (the Central
Tower moved to the crossing; see `ACAD.md`). The tool is `ACAD-files/tools/acad_stages.py` and its output is
`ACAD-files/stages.txt`.

**Frame** (as in `acad2v2_layout.py`):
- x = depth from the gate. x 0 is the gate side, facing the town square.
- z = frontage.
- A piece is 64 × 64. Piece rN covers x 64N..64N+63, and piece cM covers z 64M..64M+63.
- Plans are seen from above: gate side at the top, lake at the bottom, z 0 on the left.

---

## 0. Bottom line

- **Footprints:**
  - **Academy I** = the 4 × 4 core (256 × 256): pieces r1–r4 × c1–c4.
  - **Academy II** = Academy I plus a ring one piece wide: 6 × 6 = 384 × 384. This is his ruling D-C1006-ANS2.
  - **Academy III, proposal A** = Academy II plus one column of pieces on each shore side: 6 × 8 = 384 × 512. The gate–lake
    axis (z 191.5) stays where it is.
- **Each stage upgrades in place.** No piece ever moves. A stage only adds:
  - new pieces on reserved ground, and
  - tier versions (`_t2`, `_t3`) of standing pieces, at the same origin and rotation.

  A tier version may only build on ground that was open before, raise a roof or cap, or re-lay the furniture listed for it.
  Measured on the v2 layout:
  - **0 violations**, after one stage change: the Laboratories go to AI-1, because the Left Cloister runs through their
    ground floor.
  - **6 straddling buildings.** Their core parts are open ground until they are built, and nothing stands in the way.
  - **3 buildings need a trim of 4–6 rows** to sit inside Academy I (Q5).
- **Trigger: the town's tier** (`pw_civ_clock.js:113` TIERS). Two ladders are offered:
  - **Recommended (ladder A):** AI-1 at town I → AI-2 at town III → AII-1 at city I → AII-2 at **city III** → AIII-1 at
    metropolis I → AIII-2 at capital.
  - **Fast (ladder B):** Academy III reached at city III.
- **Land:** the *final* footprint is reserved at the first stage, the same way the crown reserves palace land at town III
  (`pw_civ_clock.js:1631–1642`).
  - Proposal A needs 448 deep (lake band included) × 512 frontage, + a 2-block margin.
  - The ruling's ~448 covers Academy II only.
- **Roles:** the new `student` and `master` roles are ruled (D-C1006-ANS2). The id `master` collides with the craft rank
  "master" (`pw_civ_people.js:291`), so another internal id is proposed (Q6).

**Why I recommend A:**
- **The site leaves only two directions free.**
  - The gate side faces the town square (K6 a), so a ring there grows into the square and the viaduct.
  - The lake side is the lake band (x 384..447), so a ring there means building on water.
  - B needs both of those; A needs neither.
  - A grows along the shore, where the lake-cliff site continues.
- **The axis stays put.** The z range −64..447 is still centred on z 191.5, so the gatehouse, viaduct, crossing tower and
  water gate stay on the axis. No piece of Academy II moves.
- **It is true to form.** Universities grow by adding colleges beside the first one; Cambridge's colleges strung along the
  River Cam are the general example (general history, not from our research doc). A concentric ring is a castle pattern;
  it was used for Academy I → II because the ruling asked for it.
- **It is cheaper to build.**
  - v1 counts 36 pieces × 5 height-band stages ≈ 180 placement beats.
  - A needs 48 pieces ≈ 240 beats; B needs 64 pieces ≈ 320.

**What the two new columns hold (PROPOSAL):**
- **c-1, the New Quad:** a second hall, House Towers V–VI, a physic garden, and an observatory. The garden and observatory
  were in v1 and dropped from v2.
- **c6, the University buildings:** a University Church with a Senate House, the printing house (it moves out of the
  Gatehouse's upper floor), and a boathouse on the shore.
- **The old precinct walls** (z 0–2 and z 381–383) stay as inner walls. Arches are cut through them by the c0 / c5
  `_t3` pieces; this is a listed re-lay, not a demolition.

## 4. In place: how a stage upgrades without demolishing the one before

1. **One frame, fixed at the first stage.**
   - The reservation fixes the frame's world origin and its rotation (0 / 90 / 180 / 270, with the gate toward the square,
     K6 a).
   - It is **never mirrored** (engine law: mirroring does not touch custom states).
   - Frame piece (i, j) is placed at origin + rot(64 i, 64 j). This is `palaceGridRot` (`pw_civ_clock.js:1509`) generalised
     from the palace's 2 × 2 to 6 × 8.
2. **Academy I is frame pieces r1–r4 × c1–c4.** Its world origin is the frame origin + rot(64, 64).
   - Academy II adds r0, r5, c0 and c5.
   - Academy III A adds c-1 and c6.
   - No piece ever moves; the frame is reserved for the final stage at the first one.
3. **A later stage places two kinds of piece:**
   - **(a)** NEW pieces on reserved ground;
   - **(b)** tier versions `_tN` of STANDING pieces, at the same origin and rotation.

   A tier version may only:
   - build on ground that was open in the previous version;
   - grow upward, replacing a roof or cap;
   - re-lay the furniture, stations and stairs listed in its stage table.

   It never cuts a wall, floor or bed of a standing building. **New verifier check R16, "monotone"** (for
   `academy_verify.py`, the castle_verify successor in v1 §8): compare version k and version k+1 of each piece cell by cell.
   Every changed non-air cell must be ground made into building, roof / cap made into a higher storey, or a listed re-lay.
4. **Straddlers are built with the ring piece.** Measured on v2:
   - House Tower I: core part (64, 64, 79, 79).
   - Dorm Range I: (80, 64, 99, 79).
   - House Tower II: (64, 304, 79, 319).
   - Dorm Range II: (80, 304, 99, 319).
   - Junior School: (100, 300, 149, 319).
   - Forecourt: (64, 100, 95, 283).

   Each one's core part goes into the core piece's `_t2`. In every case the ground is open until AII-1: **0 blockers**.
5. **Seams.** The gate–lake axis z 191.5 lies on the c2 | c3 piece seam. Any even grid puts it there, so the whole central
   building straddles c2 / c3.
   - The Twin Stair's well (x 163–168, z 189–194) crosses that seam.
   - Both pieces must therefore be placed in the same height-band stage.
   - Custom-block stairs go LAST (LC-1006-SP 3).
6. **People.** A tier version that re-lays beds hands them back the same day, as the palace's role beds do. Nobody is
   evicted by an upgrade: students move into the towers only once the towers' beds exist.

**Measured** (`stages.txt`):
- In-place violations: **0**.
  - The one found at first was the Laboratories (planned for AI-2) over the Left Cloister (AI-1). The cloister runs through
    the Labs' ground floor at z 96–99.
  - That is fixed by building the Labs at AI-1.
- Academy I outside the core: **none, once the Q5 trims are applied.** Without them, three buildings cross the core edge:
  - Library: (256, 60, 305, 99), 4 rows in c0.
  - Observatory Wing: (220, 60, 251, 95), 4 rows in c0.
  - Chapel: (260, 292, 303, 325), 6 rows in c5.
- **The Q5 trims (not applied — his call):**
  - Library → (256, 64, 305, 99): 50 × 36.
  - Observatory Wing → (220, 64, 251, 95): 32 × 32.
  - Chapel → (260, 292, 303, 319): 44 × 28.
  - The same rows come off LIBRARY upper gallery, Book cellar, Astronomer, Alchemist's Vault, CHAPEL (void), FOUNDERS'
    CRYPT and the three MASS boxes.
  - Secrets stay in their hosts: 5 at (262, 96); 10 at (300, 64), on the new edge row; 15 at (270, 296); 29 at (236, 78);

**Why A:**
- A matches the castle ladder (CT1 town III … CT4 metropolis, castle doc §9) and v1's academy ladder.
- It keeps the academy in proportion to its city:
  - Academy II's 192 students arrive at city III.
  - Academy III's 288 students arrive at metropolis I, where DENSITY is 6 people per dwelling (`:120`).
- Reaching AIII in a test world needs the forcing command below. Ladder B shows Academy III at City III, but then a ~300-student
  university sits in a city of city-class density.

**Mechanism (for the build plan, not written).**
- The tier-up runs `tierUpBody` (`pw_civ_clock.js:1997`). Its `after` list `["centres", "palace", "border", "marks"]`
  (line 2014) gains an `"academy"` step.
- The step mirrors the palace's: `if (tw.tier === PALACE_TIER && !st.palace) placePalace(…)` (line 2053).
- Each stage is built as **the academy's works**, on the palace rule: wages from the treasury and imported materials, so it
  never waits for stock.
- Stage k + 1 also waits until every piece of stage k has reached s4.
- **Witness hook (PROPOSAL, not built):** `/scriptevent pw:academy stage <AI-1|AI-2|AII-1|AII-2|AIII-1|AIII-2>` forces a
  stage at the player's position for testing. This continues the summon ruling (D-C572: "summonable by scriptevent").

## 6. Land reserve

- **When:** at the first stage, for the FINAL footprint. This is the castle-first hard requirement (castle doc §9).
  - Precedent in code: `reservePalaceLand` (`:1635`) reserves at town III. `reserveBox` (`:1632`, +2 margin) and
    `inReserve` / `boxInReserve` (`:1633–1634`) keep houses and lanes out.
  - `placePalace` re-checks whether a street grew into the reserve (`:1644–1652`).
  - The academy would carry its own `st.academyReserve` through the same three helpers.
- **How much:**

  | Footprint covered | Size (lake band included) | Area |
  |---|---|---|
  | Academy II only (the ruling's ~448) | 448 deep × 384 | 172,032 m² |
  | Academy III proposal A | 448 deep × 512 frontage (+2 margin) | 229,376 m² (+33 %) |
- **Survey:** one 160 × 160 ticking area at a time (the palace survey, LC-1005-11).
  - A 448 × 512 box is ⌈448/160⌉ × ⌈512/160⌉ = 3 × 4 = **12 windows**.
  - That is the same mechanism and size as the 640 × 640 capital survey he approved ("one ticking area at a time, ~12 save
    slots", decision_journal.md:3972).
- **Site:** a lake cliff (v1 Q8). Proposal A needs the cliff and shore to continue 64 blocks beyond the Academy II box on
  both frontage sides. The site search therefore tests 512, not 384.
