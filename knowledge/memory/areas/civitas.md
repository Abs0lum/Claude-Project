- [stated] Utopia-First: cities develop on their own first; he wants to witness the in-game development of civilization without his interference before any narrative work
- [stated] He will personally govern the creation of narrative-driven growth AFTER that witness phase
- [stated] asked to remember: "abandoned homes, lost assets, the fallout of turmoil" — the motivations behind "less than legal" activities he had not addressed when crime and discontentment were discussed; remind him when crime / discontent / narrative-driven growth is actively being developed (they are integral to narrative-driven growth)
- [stated] GALLERY direction (2026-10-04): wants a few thousand public-domain paintings in the packs so different buildings and palaces each hang unique pieces the player can identify and learn about; the city palace gets ~250 ("make it feel grand"), an equal share per subject with the most famous works as exceptions; a book item (like the naturalist's book) that identifies a work and gives the artist, the date, the artist's life, and a description, interpretation and critique — meant to be educational
- [stated] CASTLES direction (2026-10-04): wants to-scale castles and castle towns/cities where the town's infrastructure incorporates the castle; also extremely large, grand castles "more like palaces"; and, for fun, a life-like Hogwarts-style castle summonable in-game by scriptevent or structure load, with the extra rooms and passages extrapolated so the property is true to form and implied function
- [stated] WATER law (2026-10-05): "water can't be stopped, only moved" — water that touches a structure's site is to be fed into a man-made waterfall / fountain structure and funnelled down into the sewers so it exits naturally; palaces need below-ground infrastructure with multiple sewer entrances; ALL structures must include subsurface infrastructure
- [stated] castle answers (2026-10-05): moat decided by the castle's seed; a separate paid guard body recruited over time as the population rises (never summoned); four academy towers; castle-first settlements grow their castle into the grand castle-palace, the others get the palace; a paid castle household; the castle gate faces the square; the academy-castle also founds a rare academy city by itself; a BDS villager walk test on top of the offline audit
- [stated] gallery answers (2026-10-05): the FULL Gallery RP everywhere (LITE only if the phone struggles); towns hang anything 1200–1900 (the palace pre-1800); "classical" meant the classical tradition, not antiquity
- [stated] PALACE II answers (2026-10-06): palaces/castles may be enlarged freely, blueprinted off real palaces down to secret hallways, doors, staircases and underground passages; Palace II footprint 4 x 4 pieces (256 x 256); secret stairs use the narrow spiral design A; royal nursery for 4 children; hidden passages are used by the lord, family, guards and servants and are also player discovery content
- [stated] POPULATION goal (2026-10-06): towns should EVENTUALLY reach 5,000–10,000 people; the capacity must be available in the packs now (no cap), but tests won't push that far; memory issues and lag prevention at that scale are to be worked out later
- [stated] HOUSEHOLDS & DYNASTY direction (2026-10-06): grown children move out at adulthood (no longer linked to parents except through dynasty), upgrade to better homes when moving out, can then have children; heirs sell old homes after parents pass; whenever someone moves out of and/or into a home, the home is restored from its weathered state to fresh (makes jobs, spends money, keeps cities updated), and home sales replenish those costs
- [stated] TREES rule (2026-10-06): towns cleared leaves but left very long bare logs/stumps; any tree in town with fewer than 30% of its original canopy leaves must be removed entirely by the village
