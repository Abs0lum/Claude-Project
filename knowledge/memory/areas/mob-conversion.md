- [stated] Standing ruling (2026-09-27): until there is a concrete conversion tool "we personally create and verify" that can produce skeletons and animations for any mob he introduces that doesn't map correctly, all existing AbsolutRealism mob conversions are treated as "possibly flawed"
- [stated] History: the existing conversions were done using only his visual confirmation through trial and error; it took a long time to find any sort of standard; he initially began the conversions using Gemini and the original research may be lost
- [stated] A previous direct-conversion attempt produced horizontal and/or vertical gaps in mob bodies, incorrectly connected parts, or wildly visually incorrect results — the reason he wants to "be safe" with conversion work
- [stated] Goal: work out how anything gets converted so there is a repeatable standard, and research whether there are better ways to recreate Java mobs and textures on Bedrock — "are there ways to make the mobs more accurate to the Java original, is the challenge"
- [stated] Scope (2026-09-27): "only research, no building" at first; later the same day he gave GO for Phase 0 (the in-game probe/rig session) — the tool programme is authorised to proceed phase by phase
- [stated] Witness report 2026-09-27: trader llamas rendered geometrically incorrect; asked to correct them by referencing "the llamas we had that were perfect"
- [stated] Rulings on the re-conversion programme (2026-09-27): "I want everything fixed if it needs it" (no mob family left as is); chests/saddles/decor/armor layers: "Convert and double check all. Always use Patrix quality"; bone naming: "whatever bedrock REQUIRES we use; otherwise, I leave it to your discretion"
- [stated] History: the shipped mob shapes were his own in-game reconstructions — pieces moved by what was seen visually; where a mob began severely out of shape he first rebuilt it into a recognizable form with no frame of reference for front and back
- [stated] How he wants in-game test sessions run: as a script command that runs the setup commands itself while he takes screenshots between phases/mobs (so none are missed and he knows which shots to take); he verifies direction with the compass; all screenshots get uploaded to the Drive at the end
- [stated] Has Java Edition on the laptop (cannot run Java on his phone) for reference observation of the Java mobs
- [stated] Mob-size ruling (2026-09-27): creatures should sit close to real-world size to fit his "hyper realism" intent (the beetle/ant were "too large"), with a floor so they stay "highly interactable" — he took the floored scheme with the floor raised 10 %; "I don't want to lose functionality or purpose within and of the mobs"
- [stated] Size ruling update (2026-10-01): EVERY mob goes through the like-life scaling; chose curve option A — true size from 30 cm, gentle ramp below with a lower minimum (ants/termites); not cartoonishly large, closer to realistic is better; versions of the same species should sit within a close range and ratio of height and length; realistic per-cube ratios (body, head, nose) per mob to come after his next quality-elimination pass
- [stated] Standardization goal (2026-10-01): wants uniform skeletons across all mobs — bones, joints, parent/child chains, pivots/rotations, one naming language, all animations re-aligned — and the Patrix-bird flight he considers visually perfect recreated on all flying/bird-like mobs (wings split into pieces); every version of the same animal should share the same movements when he judges
- [stated] Design ruling (2026-09-28): doesn't like 3D/animated eyes on mobs — "get rid of the 3d/animated eyes on the panda or and other eyes we put on any mobs"
- [stated] Goal (2026-09-29): hundreds of different mob types; approved the "Menagerie" program — all lanes A–E (sources on hand, vanilla completion, species on shared rigs, new rigs, his own packs, and fantasy), with fantasy mobs kept in their own namespace; extinct animals are in; new-species textures generated from recipes + photo targets and approved by family; no per-biome species limit — species appear everywhere they would realistically be
- [stated] Third-party add-on rulings (2026-09-30): adding every mob from his creature add-ons, taking all animations from all packs; for every species found in 2+ packs he wants a side-by-side per pack and chooses the highest-quality version himself; the CreaturesPlus (Java) vs Patrix comparison is kept separate and announced before it is presented; dinosaurs (Jurassic pack) + the capture-orb "Pokemon" mechanic go in their own easily removable pack pair; other non-vanilla animals go into the neutral mobs pack if it fits, otherwise a new "wildlife" pack
- [stated] Mob shine ruling (2026-10-01): animals should not look shiny — sheen is "rarer than it is common", maybe certain birds; "certainly isn't true of a buffalo" (he saw some mobs too shiny after the MERS round)
- [stated] Purpose-split ruling (2026-10-02): for realism he sometimes separates a mob's or block's purpose into two categories — mobs scaled below original size that can be hostile get a normal-size neutral/friendly version (for variety and realism, bite half a heart) and a larger, easily visible neutral/hostile version for encounters (2.5 hearts damage) that spawns less often than the friendly one
