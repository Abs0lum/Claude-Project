## §8 · LESSONS

**L-STRICT-RBW (CANDIDATE, D-C286 — his enderman jaw c03 + 10 "unknown variable" errors):** a client entity with a modern min_engine_version drops every Molang statement that reads a never-set variable; frame-to-frame recurrences and reads before the first write need `(v.x ?? 0)`, or the chain stays unset forever. Raising min_engine_version (e.g. to win a PREC tie) can switch a working entity into this mode — run RBW first.

**L-RC-DEFAULT-HIDE (CANDIDATE, D-C285 — his silverfish "invisible, but I can see his shadow"):** a vanilla render controller whose part_visibility starts `{"*": "0"}` and allow-lists the vanilla bone names hides a converted model ENTIRELY (only the shadow draws). Every converted model whose vanilla RC carries part_visibility gets its own RC; gate RCV.

**L-QQ-DIRECT (CANDIDATE, D-C285 — his R8 content log):** Bedrock's `??` needs a DIRECT variable (`v.x`, `t.x`, `c.x`) on its left; a struct member (`variable.squid.swim_rotation`) or a query is rejected and the WHOLE animation is dropped. molang_lint enforces it.

**L-FMT-SCRIPTS (CANDIDATE, D-C285 — his R8 content log):** a format 1.8.0 client entity rejects `scripts.animate` and `scripts.initialize` ("child … not valid here") and silently plays nothing new; play extra animations through its `animation_controllers` list (seed variables in a state's on_entry). Gate FMT.

**L-BAKE-WET (CANDIDATE, D-C285 — his tadpole "on its side"):** a JEM that branches on `is_in_water` must be baked in the branch the mob rests in — aquatic mobs in water (the tadpole's dry branch is a 70° flop). convb.AQUATIC lists them.

**L-HOLD-POINT (CANDIDATE, D-C285 — his "bow not quite set in the hand"):** held items and the vanilla attachables hang on the geometry's rightItem / leftItem bone; a converted humanoid needs them at the vanilla-frame point (skeleton [-6, 15, 1] on a 12-px arm) — at the fingertips the grip sits 3 px low, and with no bone at all the item has nowhere to go.

**L-SCALED-BOXUV (CANDIDATE, D-C284 — his spider witness + 90-face census):** box UV derives every face rectangle from the cube's SIZE, so a cube whose size was scaled (a baked part scale) samples a shrunken layout — faces reach into the sheet's empty gaps and draw as exact square holes. Never scale a box-UV cube; freeze its unscaled layout as explicit per-face UV first (`convb.boxuv_to_faces`, round-trip exact against Blockbench's box layout).

**L-ROT-DIR (VERIFIED 09-27, D-C255 — SUPERSEDES the CONVERSION-STANDARD's §2.1/§2.3/§3.1(X,Z)/§3.2/§4.2, which are tagged SUPERSEDED BY L-ROT-DIR in place; full form + CONV-1 + CEM-RT in `MOB-CONVERSION-RESEARCH-2026-09-27.md`):** a Bedrock bone rotation +90 about X maps a point (y, z) about the pivot to y′ = p_y + (z − p_z), z′ = p_z − (y − p_y) — the cube's local +Y goes to −Z (FRONT) and its local +Z goes to +Y (UP). §4.2 states the opposite (local +Y → +Z back, local +Z → −Y down). Evidence for the candidate: vanilla `wolf_armor.geo.json` (tail 55 / body 90 / upperBody −90 only make a wolf this way), the Java ModelQuadruped → vanilla cow `bind_pose_rotation [90,0,0]` conversion (body lands at y 12–22 this way), our witnessed cow + wolf geometries (on their legs this way; 4 cubes into / above them the other way), the roof family (D-C197) and his 09:57 trader llama shot (the neck hangs below the body only this way). If confirmed: SUPERSEDES the §4.2 direction table only — its UV / face-naming rules stand (the 'up' face still becomes a world side face; only WHICH side flips).
