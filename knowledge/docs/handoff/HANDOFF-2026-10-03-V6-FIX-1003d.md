## 2. What 1.3.210 fixes (GS-1, the "two odd street cells")
- **Mechanism (server-proven):** `layPlannedStreet` decided "run cell or jog cell" from the PREVIOUS cell only. The first cell of every run after a jog looked like a jog, so it was laid as a centred cross and its two body cells were never laid — a hole / a 1-block bump at every run start, on both street axes. The forest village of run 0.0.17 showed it at (40,34)/(41,34); the probe reproduced it at (68,60)/(69,60) and (88,111)/(89,111) at the same square, present from founding.
- **Fix:** the lay geometry is now one pure function, `LAND.layShapes(cells, i, axis)`: a run cell lays its 3-wide body on the far side of the key row/column; a jog cell (and every road cell) lays a 3-wide cross across its travel; a run's first cell after a jog lays **both** (the corridor turns the corner as a solid L). Unit test `tools/bp02_src/test_lay.mjs` 169/169 (z-axis, x-axis, roads, every key of a whole street).
- **Also in 210:** the TREEGROW sapling registry is saved compact (type / dimension as table indexes) in numbered 30,000-char chunks (`pw:treegrow:n` + `:k`) — the 400-entry cap could have passed the 32,767-char dynamic-property limit; a 1.3.209 world's single-property registry still loads.
