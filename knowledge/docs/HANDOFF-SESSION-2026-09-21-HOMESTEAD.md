## §3 · WHAT WAS BUILT (mechanisms, not pseudocode — the files are the spec)
**Units:** geometry in cubes (1/16 block); textures 128 px/block → **1 cube = 8 px**. Local **−z = the face toward the placer** (`cardinal_direction` + `y_rotation_offset 180`, 'north' → rotation [0,0,0] — copied from roof45, witnessed).

**`pw:hearth_<m>`** (53): states `pw:phase` cold/fueled/lit/embers × cardinal. Geometry per phase (`geometry.pw_hearth_<phase>`): 2-cube back skin at +z (outer face = material, fire face = `pw_hearth_soot`), hearth floor 16×1×14 (soot), bed 12×1×8 (`pw_hearth_ash` cold/fueled, `pw_hearth_embers` lit/embers — emissive MERS), 4 crossed logs 12×3×3 (`pw_hearth_log` = oak_log_v0) on fueled/lit, 2 crossed 12×10 alpha-test quads on **`fire_0`** (RP-04's Patrix fire flipbook) on lit. Cheeks = the neighbouring wall blocks' faces (free). Collision/selection full cell; `light_dampening 15`; `light_emission` lit 15 / embers 7. Custom component `pw:hearth` (onPlace registers; onPlayerInteract = status). **Vanilla flint & steel would set fire to the cell in front** → `world.beforeEvents.playerInteractWithBlock` cancels fuel/igniter uses on hearths and runs ours next tick.
Fuel machine (`pw_homestead_logic.js`, pure, 11 Node tests): UNIT = ¼ day = 6000 ticks; planks 1 · log/wood 2 · coal/charcoal 4; cold+fuel → **fueled** (waits for flint & steel); fueled + flint → **lit** (durability −1; fire charge single-use); lit + fuel → extends (cap 16 units = 4 days ahead, item refused beyond); embers + fuel → **lit** without flint; lit expires → **embers for 2 × the whole lit phase** → cold. Ledger: world dynamic properties `pw:hearth|<dim>|x,y,z` with absolute-time deadlines (fire keeps burning while the chunk is unloaded and the world open — a realism choice; while the world is closed nothing burns).
**Smoke** (40-tick cycle, only with a player within 48): crackle `block.campfire.crackle` every 4 s; **chute walk** up from the hearth: every cell must be `pw:flue_*` until air → exhaust `pw:chimney_smoke` above the top flue; any other block → blocked → **room** = bounded flood-fill from the cell in front of the hearth (≤400 non-solid cells, radius 12, cached 30 s; bigger = outdoors, smoke dissipates); **level 0..15** (+1/cycle blocked, −1 otherwise); `pw:room_smoke` particles (block-colliding, slow rise, 9–15 s, count ∝ level, ceiling-biased) · fog `pw:smoke_room` pushed on players inside at level ≥ 8, popped < 6 · level ≥ 12: "you cough" + slowness I. `/scriptevent pw:home reflow | status | smoke <0-15> | clear`.
**`pw:flue_<m>`** (53): `minecraft:geometry.full_block` (`*` side, `up/down` top key); state `pw:cap` = nothing above → `geometry.pw_flue_cap` (body + 18×2×18 rim ring + soot throat). Resolver: onPlace (self + the flue below), any place/break above a flue re-resolves it. Structure loads → `reflow`.
**`pw:wall_<m>`** (53): `full_block`; `pw:inner` = 16 interior finishes (oak/spruce/birch/dark oak planks, smooth stone, stone bricks, cobblestone, bricks, mud bricks, packed mud, white terracotta, terracotta, calcite, polished tuff, quartz, polished deepslate); permutations = 4 directions × 16 → the material_instances face key named by the state (no `transformation`, so the unwitnessed "face keys rotate with transformation" question never arises). Inner faces the placer; **empty-hand tap cycles the finish, tap with a stick flips**. Wood outers are flammable.
**`pw:rafter45_oak/spruce`**: one 4×4×22.6 box rotated **[−45,0,0]** about the cell centre — high end toward the placer — stripped-log keys; **`collision_box: false`**, selection full, `light_dampening 0`, flammable; script: slowness II (~70 %) refreshed every 10 ticks while a player's feet or head cell is a rafter.
**Recipes:** hearth = 8 × material around a **campfire**; flue = two columns of 3 (→3); wall = column of 3 (→3); rafter = diagonal of 3 planks (→3). Tags `crafting_table` + `pw:builders_table`. Names via `texts/en_US.lang` (RP-04 had no lang file — see §6).
