### 3b. Motion that RP-07 switches off in the game (SHIMS)

RP-07 replaces 55 of the game's own animations with do-nothing versions, a leftover of the April conversion. Where our own
animations stand in, that is harmless. Motion that is **lost with nothing replacing it**:

| Mob | What's lost |
|---|---|
| **enderman** | Walk swing and attack swing. The game's own enderman file runs on RP-07's enderman model. |
| **squid, glow squid** | Tentacle pulse, and the body tilting toward where it swims. |
| **dolphin** | Body pitch as it swims. |
| **tadpole** | The game's tail wag. Our small ambient wiggle stays. |
| **parrot** | The dance. |
| **silverfish, giant** (RP-06), **parched**, legacy zombie villager | Their only motion. |

Plan (next RP-07): drop the do-nothing versions that cause a loss, and give squid, glow squid and dolphin real animations fitted
to the Patrix models. That is part of the aquatic wave. The horse/llama "setup" do-nothing versions **stay**; they stop the
game's setup poses from bending the Patrix neck and body.

### 3e. The Java parent/child structure (his "Java does something different" theory, confirmed in a new place)

In Java, some model parts hang off another part: a hat on a head, a head on a body, the whole bee on a hidden root. The Patrix
file lists all parts flat, so the converter placed each child as if its parent never moved. Tonight's fix covers the piglin
family's hat/head and the bee's root. **Candidates to check next:** camel (head under body), chicken (beak and wattle under head),
frog (head under body), axolotl (head, legs and tail under body), dolphin (fins and tail under body). This matters only where
the parent has a rest tilt or offset that the Patrix animation sets.
